## The Front Page

### Oxford Quantum Circuits closes GBP 260m Series C

- 3 June 2026 | Sources: [OQC press release](https://oqc.tech/company/newsroom/series-c), [OQC LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/oqc_quantumcomputing-deeptechinvestment-quantuminvestment-activity-7467836860967464960-BTUH), and [Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/oxford-quantum-circuits-raises-260m-in-oversubscribed-series-c-funding-round/)
- OQC closed an oversubscribed GBP 260m ($350m) Series C led by Bullhound Capital. The company describes it as Europe's largest private funding round for a quantum-computing company.
- The investor group includes the British Business Bank, Fynveur, COFIDES, RCM Private Markets Fund, Alpha Edison, Fulcrum Asset Management, Pentland Ventures, Magdalen College Oxford, Adaptive Capital Partners, Firgun Ventures, 18 West and Oxford Capital, alongside existing investors.
- The capital is intended to expand deployed systems in the UK, US, Japan and Spain, deepen R&D, and advance OQC's roadmap toward commercially useful fault-tolerant computing.
- Why it matters: this is the week's dominant portfolio event and a significant test of whether European quantum companies can turn large private rounds into deployed enterprise infrastructure. Confidence: High.

### Grey Wolf reports Phase 1b responses across six tumour types

- 2 June 2026 company-page publication; release body datelined 22 May 2026 | Sources: [Grey Wolf Therapeutics](https://www.greywolftherapeutics.com/news/greywolf-therapeutics-reports-durable-clinical-responses-with-first-in-class-oral-erap1-inhibitor-grwd5769-across-six-solid-tumor-types) and [company LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/greywolf-therapeutics_greywolf-therapeutics-at-asco26-emitt-1-activity-7467496949789638656-E6L4)
- Data presented at ASCO from six Phase 1b EMITT-1 expansion cohorts showed objective response rates of 13% to 36% for oral ERAP1 inhibitor GRWD5769 combined with cemiplimab. Grey Wolf reported durable clinical benefit rates of 18% to 55%.
- The company said multiple patients remained progression-free for more than six months, treatment was well tolerated, and no safety signal had emerged. It is advancing Stage 2 cohort expansions to inform a randomised Phase 2 study.
- Why it matters: the evidence is early and uncontrolled, but activity across several immunotherapy-resistant tumour types is a meaningful clinical signal for a first-in-class mechanism. Confidence: High on the reported data; clinical interpretation remains preliminary.

### FluoRok wins an additional GBP 1.2m DRIVE35 grant

- 5 June 2026 | Sources: [FluoRok](https://www.fluorok.com/news/fluorok-secures-1-2m-drive35-grant-to-accelerate-commercialisation-of-safer-cost-competitive-sustainable-hf-free-li-ion-electrolyte-salt-production/) and [company LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/fluorok_cleantech-innovation-advancedmanufacturing-activity-7468639594033500160-nSd4)
- FluoRok received an additional GBP 1.2m feasibility-study grant through the UK government's DRIVE35 programme.
- The work will support commercialisation of a hydrogen-fluoride-free route to lithium hexafluorophosphate, a key lithium-ion battery electrolyte salt, with the project expected to begin in July 2026.
- Why it matters: this is non-dilutive validation tied to a strategic battery supply-chain bottleneck and gives FluoRok another funded step toward commercial process scale-up. Confidence: High.

### Refeyn and Bio-Techne launch a four-hour biologics workflow

- 4 June 2026 | Sources: [Bio-Techne investor release](https://investors.bio-techne.com/press-releases/detail/534/biotechne-and-refeyn-close-critical-gap-in-bispecific) and [Refeyn LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/refeyn_bispecific-antibodies-are-among-the-most-activity-7468250175505874945-Srto)
- Refeyn and Bio-Techne introduced an integrated workflow combining MauriceFlex charge-variant fractionation with Refeyn's TwoMP mass-photometry platform.
- The companies say the workflow correlates charge heterogeneity with molecular weight and aggregation at single-molecule resolution in about four hours, using nanogram-level samples.
- Why it matters: this links Refeyn's instrument directly into a partner workflow for complex biologics and biosimilar development, where sample scarcity and characterisation time can be material constraints. Confidence: High.
