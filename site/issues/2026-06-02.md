# Issue 001: OSE Portfolio News - 2 June 2026

Run date: 2 June 2026. Coverage window: 26 May to 2 June 2026, with a short catch-up section for older but strategically material May items surfaced during this first run. Roster refreshed from the Oxford Science Enterprises portfolio page: 81 companies checked.

## Executive Summary

- Genomics launched Mystra AI, a conversational human-genetics platform for target discovery and validation, with named adoption signals from AWS, Novo Nordisk, BridgeBio, Relation Therapeutics, and the BioIndustry Association.
- Caudal Energy raised GBP 4.3m, led by Oxford Science Enterprises and Empirical Ventures, to take its fin-inspired tidal technology toward full-scale Strangford Lough testing and a 2028 commercial target.
- Archangel Lightworks completed TERRA-M field trials and, separately, Dstl reported the UK's first successful optical space downlink using Archangel's deployable ground station.
- Theolytics will present its US Phase 1 OCTOPOD-IP THEO-260 ovarian cancer trial as a Trials in Progress poster at ASCO 2026.
- Gilead has stopped further development of GS-0272, the former MiroBio BTLA agonist MB272, a negative read-through for that acquired program and the broader BTLA agonist modality.
- Fractile's USD 220m raise, Quantum Motion's USD 160m Series C, and ODQA's high-temperature solar heat milestone are older than this weekly window but are included as first-run catch-up items because they are material OSE portfolio signals.

- 27 May 2026 | Startup harnessing energy from tides raises GBP 4.3m | Source: [UKTN](https://www.uktech.news/energy/startup-harnessing-energy-from-tides-raises-4-3m-20260527), with additional coverage from [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/05/27/caudal-energy-raises-ps43m-to-scale-fin-inspired-tidal-power-technology/)

### Archangel Lightworks

- 27 May 2026 | World's smallest deployable operational optical ground station proves capability in trials | Source: [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/worlds-smallest-deployable-operational-optical-ground-station-proves-capability-in-successful-trials-302782355.html) and [GOV.UK / Dstl](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/dstl-achieves-uks-first-optical-downlink-from-space)

- Why it matters: This is technical validation in a defence-backed setting, with the company saying TERRA-M units and ground-station-as-a-service contracts are already being purchased and delivered.
- Tags: defence tech, space, optical communications, field trial, customer traction. Confidence: High.
