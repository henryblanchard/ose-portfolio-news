## The Front Page

### Sitryx completes enrollment in Phase 1b SYX-5219 atopic-dermatitis trial

- 22 June 2026 | Sources: [Sitryx via GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315158/0/en/sitryx-completes-enrollment-in-phase-1b-trial-of-first-in-class-oral-pkm2-modulator-syx-5219-in-atopic-dermatitis.html) and [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07558668](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07558668)
- Sitryx said enrollment has completed ahead of schedule in its Phase 1b study of SYX-5219, a first-in-class oral PKM2 modulator being tested in adults with moderate to severe atopic dermatitis.
- The company says topline data are expected in the fourth quarter of 2026 and that the trial is designed to assess safety, tolerability and initial efficacy. ClinicalTrials.gov independently identifies NCT07558668 as a Sitryx-sponsored study of SYX-5219 in healthy volunteers and patients with moderate to severe atopic dermatitis.
- Why it matters: this is a concrete clinical-execution milestone for an immunometabolism company whose programme is now moving from mechanistic promise toward patient data. Confidence: High on enrollment status as company-reported and registry-linked; efficacy remains unproven until data readout.

### ORCA Computing and Toyota Tsusho place a photonic quantum system inside a Japanese enterprise

- 16 June 2026 | Sources: [HPCwire carrying ORCA release](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/orca-computing-and-toyota-tsusho-deploy-quantum-system-to-enterprise-customer-in-japan/), [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/06/16/orca-computing-and-toyota-tsusho-deploy-first-quantum-system-to-enterprise-customer-in-japan/), and [ORCA Computing site index](https://orcacomputing.com/)
- ORCA Computing announced the deployment of its PT Series photonic quantum computer to a major enterprise customer in Japan with support from Toyota Tsusho Corporation.
- The release says the PT-2 system was installed in under one week in a live enterprise environment, is intended to support hybrid quantum-AI applications across manufacturing and optimisation workloads, and is scheduled for upgrade to ORCA's PT-3 platform later in 2026.
- Why it matters: unlike many quantum-computing announcements that remain at partnership or lab-demo level, this is framed as an installed private-sector enterprise system. The end customer is unnamed, so adoption depth and workload value still need follow-up. Confidence: Medium-High.

### First Light Fusion demonstrates VIPER orbital-impact velocities at Texas A&M

- 17 June 2026 | Sources: [First Light Fusion](https://firstlightfusion.com/media/first-light-fusion-successfully-recreates-orbital-impact-velocities-at-texas-am-engineering-experiment-station/) and [AZoQuantum](https://www.azoquantum.com/News.aspx?newsID=11170)
- First Light Fusion said it completed the first experimental campaign of its VIPER velocity amplifier at the Texas A&M Engineering Experiment Station.
- The company says VIPER achieved projectile velocities above 12 kilometres per second in the facility, recreating orbital impact speeds relevant to satellite and spacecraft shielding research. AZoQuantum carried the same technical milestone in-window.
- Why it matters: the result is not a fusion-energy demonstration, but it is a useful hardware validation signal for First Light's high-velocity platform and may broaden the company's near-term relevance into space-impact testing. Confidence: High on the company-reported test; independent technical validation was not identified.

### Seloxium named in UK critical-minerals funding package

- 22 June 2026 | Sources: [UK Government](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-to-secure-critical-minerals-boosting-economic-resilience-and-cutting-reliance-on-imports) and [Teesside Live](https://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/teesside-news/teessides-wilton-centre-spotlighted-50m-29411458)
- The UK Government announced a GBP 50m package for critical-minerals projects and listed Seloxium among the backed projects.
- The announcement frames the package around reducing import reliance and strengthening domestic supply chains. Regional coverage separately highlighted the Wilton Centre and Teesside as part of the critical-minerals push.
- Why it matters: Seloxium's technology sits squarely in a policy-backed bottleneck: recovering and refining strategically important metals. The government source confirms inclusion, but project-level award size, delivery milestones and commercial counterparties still need direct follow-up. Confidence: High on inclusion in the package; medium on project-level implications.
