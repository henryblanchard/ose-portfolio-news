## The Front Page

### FluoRok wins EUR2.5m EIC Accelerator grant for European battery-materials scale-up

- 2 July 2026 | Sources: [FluoRok](https://www.fluorok.com/news/fluorok-secures-e2-5m-eic-accelerator-funding-to-scale-critical-battery-materials-technology-in-europe/) and [FluoRok LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/fluorok_batteries-energytransition-eic-activity-7478389582863007745-FmEh)
- FluoRok said it has been awarded a EUR2.5m European Innovation Council Accelerator grant to support scale-up of its technology for producing critical fluorochemicals used in lithium-ion batteries.
- The company says its HF-free route can produce fluorochemicals such as LiPF6 for localised European supply chains, with the funding directed toward manufacturing capability, customers and suppliers.
- Why it matters: this is the week's clearest portfolio-company milestone. The grant sits directly in Europe's battery-materials sovereignty theme and follows FluoRok's earlier UK DRIVE35 funding, giving the company a second public non-dilutive scale-up signal in a strategically watched supply chain. Confidence: High on award and company-stated use of funds; medium on cost, sustainability and supply-chain performance claims pending independent operating data.

### NavLive releases NavLive 2.0 with Site View and upgraded scan processing

- 30 June 2026 | Sources: [NavLive](https://navlive.ai/news/navlive-2-0-release/), [NavLive release notes](https://navlive.ai/release-notes/) and [NavLive LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/navlive_a-faster-clearer-way-to-scan-introducing-activity-7477750017747943425-3vgV)
- NavLive released NavLive 2.0.0, describing it as its largest update so far, with a redesigned Portal, new Site View, improved scan upload and processing, better point-cloud colourisation and additional reliability fixes.
- Site View lets users revisit completed scans from a top-down floor plan, linked original image capture and 3D point-cloud view. The company says the release also moves Scan2BIM to an offline deep-learning method for cleaner wall-feature recognition.
- Why it matters: this is not just a marketing post around an existing scanner. It is a product-workflow release aimed at the practical handoff problem in AEC scanning: helping someone who was not on site interpret the scan, compare data and produce accurate drawings. Confidence: High on release and stated features; medium on performance claims until customer evidence appears.

## Specialist Desks

### OQC hardware joins qBraid's QUEST quantum-education programme

- 2 July 2026 | Sources: [qBraid](https://www.qbraid.com/blog-posts/qbraid-launches-quest-to-bring-real-quantum-hardware-into-classrooms-at-universities), [QUEST programme page](https://quest.qbraid.com/) and [OQC LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/oqc_quantumcomputing-quantumeducation-quantumhardware-activity-7478417264178933760-lGJy)
- qBraid launched QUEST, a Quantum University Education and Support Track that it says will give more than 1,200 students at 40 universities access to 25-plus quantum devices during the 2026-27 academic year.
- OQC said its superconducting quantum computers are part of the programme, giving students hands-on access to production hardware through qBraid.
- Why it matters: this is a channel and ecosystem-development signal rather than a revenue-disclosed customer win. For OQC, the useful signal is continued placement of its hardware inside third-party quantum software and education infrastructure after its recent financing visibility. Confidence: Medium-High.
