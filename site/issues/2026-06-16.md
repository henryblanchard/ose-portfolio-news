## The Front Page

### Caristo Diagnostics joins the World Economic Forum's 2026 Technology Pioneers

- 15 June 2026 | Sources: [Caristo Diagnostics via PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/caristo-diagnostics-selected-by-world-economic-forum-as-a-2026-technology-pioneer-302800384.html) and [World Economic Forum](https://www.weforum.org/press/2026/06/new-technology-pioneers-are-building-the-infrastructure-for-the-next-era-of-ai-96a8d3e248/)
- Caristo said it was selected as a 2026 Technology Pioneer by the World Economic Forum, placing it among 100 early-stage companies chosen from 23 countries.
- The company said the recognition centred on CaRi-Heart, its AI-enabled analysis for coronary inflammation on cardiac CT. The WEF independently lists Caristo among the 2026 cohort and describes the group as companies building infrastructure for the next era of AI.
- Why it matters: this is a clean third-party validation signal for a portfolio company that has been building clinical, reimbursement and regulatory momentum in cardiovascular imaging. Confidence: High.

### Natcap launches an Opportunities module for nature-related capital allocation

- 9 June 2026 | Sources: [Natcap newsroom](https://natcapresearch.com/newsroom)
- Natcap announced an Opportunities module that it says helps organisations identify, assess and prioritise nature-related opportunities and mitigation actions across sites, sourcing regions, commodities and supply chains.
- The company positions the release as an expansion from nature-risk measurement into investment and action prioritisation. The newsroom description frames it as a science-based workflow for turning nature data into commercial decision support.
- Why it matters: the release extends Natcap from disclosure and risk tooling toward budget-allocation and operational planning, which is where many enterprise climate-and-nature tools are still trying to prove practical value. Confidence: High on the launch; commercial adoption remains to be demonstrated.

## Product And Research Desk

### Prolific publishes a 100,000-plus comparison update on HUMAINE

- 11 June 2026 | Sources: [Prolific report](https://www.prolific.com/resources/state-of-humaine-june-2026), [OpenReview paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=kVaE2kYjtV), and [HUMAINE dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ProlificAI/humaine-evaluation-dataset)
- Prolific's June state-of-project report says HUMAINE has now covered 51 models, 116,536 classified conversations, 50,769 decisive head-to-head votes and 23,707 participants across the UK and US.
- The company says preference prediction still appears capped at roughly 66%, demographic variables explain only about 1% of judging variation, and crowd preferences favour substantive answers over flattering ones by about 2.5 to 1.
- Why it matters: this is one of the more concrete public data assets in the portfolio's AI-evaluation slice, and it strengthens Prolific's positioning around human preference measurement rather than generic panel access. Confidence: High on the disclosed dataset and report; broader implications for model-training practice remain interpretive.

### Kneu Health surfaces real-world Parkinson's monitoring evidence from ABN and AAN

- 11 June 2026 company recap; underlying AAN interview published 11 May 2026 | Sources: [Kneu Health](https://kneu.com/news/a-month-of-milestones-kneu-health-at-abn-and-aan) and [NeurologyLive](https://www.neurologylive.com/view/exploring-use-telehealth-parkinson-disease-dementia-monitoring-kinan-muhammed-md-phd)
- Kneu used a June company update to recap real-world Parkinson's monitoring evidence presented at major neurology meetings. The company said one AAN dataset covered 726 patients across 13 UK and US sites with more than 800,000 digital measures.
- Kneu reported that 53% of consultations used dashboard data for medication optimisation, clinicians saved an average of 20% of consultation time, and 93% of patients found the technology easy to use. NeurologyLive separately quoted co-founder Kinan Muhammed in May on the clinical promise of smartphone-based remote monitoring for Parkinson's disease and dementia.
- Why it matters: the evidence is still company-reported and conference-led, but it is more decision-useful than a routine event post because it packages multicentre usage, workflow and patient-engagement metrics into a deployment narrative. Confidence: Medium-High.
